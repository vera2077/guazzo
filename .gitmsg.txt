fix: uiux-r10 — the popover owns its keys, the ruler leaves with its tool, the banner clears

Six findings from the round-10 interface review.

The colour popover opened on top of New Fill Layer, but the dialog's capture
handler owned Escape, Tab and Enter: Escape cancelled the whole dialog, Tab
jumped to controls behind the still-open popover, and Enter in the hex field
submitted the dialog. The handler now yields those three keys while a
.color-pop is open.

The Ruler kept its measurement drawn after the tool changed — the line, the end
ticks and the px/angle chip stayed over the next picture until Escape. It now
clears in highlight() like the sampler's samples, with a smoke assertion that
switches to Brush and back and expects the bar to be empty (probed red).

The help panel said "Paint Bucket (G)" and "Focus (R)"; the keys are Shift+G
and Shift+S, and the labels already said so. The guide strings now match the
keymap instead of maintaining a second, wrong table.

A transient autosave failure set the persistent red banner, and a later success
never cleared it — the banner stayed for the rest of the session. statusError
now records its owner, autosave claims its messages, and autosaveSucceeded
clears only its own.

Brush Settings: inert sections were greyed and skipped by the arrows, but Space
still called flip() and toggled a setting with no effect. flip() refuses inert
sections now.

Menu bar: the ArrowLeft/Right comment claimed focus lands on the first command;
the code (and the smoke contract) keeps focus on the title and opens the menu as
it lands. The comment now describes what happens.
