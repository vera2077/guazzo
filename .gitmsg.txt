feat: Histogram panel — the composite's distribution, drawn when it changes

The coverage plan listed Histogram among the panels Guazzo did not have. It is
the cheapest of the missing panels and the one a painter reaches for first, so
it is the first one built.

histogramOf walks the composited RGBA buffer, ignores transparent pixels (a
hidden layer must not shape the graph) and counts red, green and blue plus BT.601
luma. The luma helper lives in the engine, not the panel, so its rounding is
asserted: 76.245 must land in bucket 76, and adding 500 before a caller's own
Math.round puts it in 77.

The card draws a 236×92 canvas with the three channels at sqrt scale (the tails
stay readable without flattening the peak) and a white luma outline. The refresh
closure is guarded by the flat cache's generation, so render() can call it on
every pointermove — a 16 MP composite is 64 MB to walk and must not be walked per
frame. It reads the cache rather than the document on purpose: the histogram
panel does not enter the coupling metric's document state.

The smoke asserts the card exists, the canvas is the size the CSS expects, and
the stats line carries a measured mean — the last one fails when the render call
is removed, so it cannot pass for a card that draws nothing.
