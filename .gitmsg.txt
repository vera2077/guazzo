feat: Colour Range, the Select menu entry that only needed a tolerance

Photoshop's Colour Range builds a selection from a colour and a fuzziness
instead of from an existing selection, which is why it belongs beside Grow and
Similar rather than in the Modify submenu. `colourRangeSelection` scans the
active layer, skips transparent pixels, and uses the same channel-max distance
the wand uses; the dialog exposes 0-200 like Photoshop and says what it measures.

Engine test: a pixel five levels away survives 40 % fuzz but not 1 %, and a
far-away colour is never selected. Coverage table row and progress log updated.
