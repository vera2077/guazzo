fix: r9 — the budgets, the blend, the geometry and the LUT keep to what the reader accepts

Nine review findings from code-r9 and sec-r9, plus the uiux-r9 batch that was
already in the tree.

Autosave wrote snapshots its own reader refused. The writer checked tiles and
bytes but not the tab count, so a session with more than 32 tabs reported
"saved" and recovered nothing on the next launch. The writer now refuses before
encoding, keeps every tab dirty, and names the limit.

An adjustment layer with a non-normal blend mode baked its opacity into alpha:
the composite path multiplies coverage by the layer opacity and compositePixel
writes that product back into the alpha channel. A Multiply levels layer over a
50 %-alpha pixel produced alpha 255 — every soft edge, mask rim and antialiased
boundary turned opaque. Colour adjustments now blend against an opaque backdrop
and restore the original coverage, with a semi-transparent test that fails
without the restore.

Rotate, flip and Image Size wrote the full destination rect, which allocates
every intersecting tile: a 4096² document with three nodes jumped to 768 tile
slots and could no longer be saved at the 512 budget. The remap now reads only
allocated source tiles, maps their corners, and leaves empty destination tiles
unallocated — proven by a rotation test that would allocate all nine tiles the
old way and allocates at most two now.

Duplicate Layer dropped the parent (a copy made inside a group landed at the
root and stayed visible when the group was hidden) and the text recipe (a
duplicated text layer could never be re-edited). Perspective Crop skipped masks
on every non-pixel layer, so a masked levels layer applied to the whole cropped
canvas.

Security: a .cube file could be larger than its declared table (parseCube
collected every row before checking, and the picker read the whole file), so the
parser now caps input at 16 MB and stops as soon as the table is provably too
long; the PSD colour-lookup record over 16 MB is admitted instead of decoded;
raster sniffing reads 256 KiB so a JPEG's SOF after EXIF is found, gains a WebP
branch (VP8X/VP8/VP8L), and the brush-tip path sniffs before decoding. The Setup
builder now verifies every packed file against the release's SHA256SUMS.txt,
requires and performs signing when RELEASE_REQUIRE_SIGNED=1, and records the
Setup's own hash.

uiux-r9: Shift+C selects Count (Crop matched "C" first), solid fills can reach
their colour swatch (stopsWrap was hidden, so every solid fill painted white),
D resets the background as well as the foreground, greyed palette rows explain
themselves on click, palette rows stop repeating their shortcut, and [ / ] keeps
the Brush Settings tip size in step.
