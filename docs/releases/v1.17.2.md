# Guazzo 1.17.2 - Brush Settings leads the brush bar

The panel opened by this button changes what every other control on the brush bar
means: Size becomes the base the jitter applies to, Hardness the edge the scatter
spreads. A control with that reach belongs at the start of the row, where the eye
looks first, not at the end behind Airbrush and Pressure.

## What changed

- `buildBrushOptions()` creates the Brush Settings button before the tip picker,
  so it is the leftmost control on the brush bar on every rebuild.
- The smoke asserts the position, not just presence: `options.children[0]` must be
  the Brush Settings button.

## Verification

- Probe: placing one control ahead of it turns the suite red with "Brush Settings
  must be the leftmost control on the brush bar"; restored, it is green.
- Full gate green (72 files / 735 tests), pins, smoke, deterministic single-file
  build.
