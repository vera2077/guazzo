## Guazzo 1.13.0

A stylus now bends the brush instead of only changing its size.

Pressure has driven dab radius since the first release, through a fixed mapping
(0.35 + 0.65 × pressure). That mapping is still there as the default; what is
new is that the artist can say what pressure *means* for three attributes.

- A **Pressure** button next to the brush options opens three small graphs:
  size, opacity and flow. Each has three knots, at 0 %, 50 % and 100 %
  pressure; drag a knot up or down and the line between knots is what the brush
  reads.
- **Size** starts on the diagonal — a default that reproduces the old behaviour
  exactly, so nothing changes for anyone who never opens the dialog.
- **Opacity** and **flow** start flat at 100 %. A pressure-sensitive opacity
  curve is a choice, not a surprise: a mouse reports a constant and must keep
  painting as it always did.
- The curves are stored (`guazzo.pressure.v1`) and loaded at start. A hostile or
  broken stored value falls back knot by knot, never to a NaN that would reach
  `ctx.font` or a dab radius.

### Where it applies

Brush and eraser, including the airbrush ticks. The retouch tools (dodge, burn,
sponge, focus, clone) keep their own falloff and their own strength controls;
giving them curves means sharing their dab loop with the brush, which is a
larger change than this release and is not implied by the button.

### Verification

- 68 test files / 693 tests, 86/86 pins, gate green.
- Unit: the default size curve reproduces the old mapping at 0, 0.5 and 1;
  straight-line interpolation between knots; a flat curve ignores pressure;
  junk curves are sanitised knot by knot; storage round-trips and a throwing or
  corrupt store falls back to defaults.
- Smoke, executed rather than grepped: the Pressure control exists; the dialog
  shows exactly three curves named size, opacity, flow; dragging the middle
  size knot to the top saves a value above 90 %; untouched opacity and flow stay
  flat; confirming closes that dialog. Probes: refusing to move the knot fails
  "the middle size knot must be saved near 100 % (got 0.5)"; not persisting
  fails the saved-value assertions.
