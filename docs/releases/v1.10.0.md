# Guazzo 1.10.0

A dual brush, and a slider that finally means what it says.

## What changed

- **Second tip (dual brush).** Next to the brush options there is now a second
  tip: `none`, chalk, spray or square, plus a size. Its alpha multiplies each
  dab's coverage, so the stroke breaks up where the second tip is dark instead
  of being one flat disc. A smaller second tip textures only the middle of the
  stroke and leaves the rim alone.
- **Hardness was inverted for the brush and eraser.** The formula in
  `src/engine/paint.ts` computed the solid core as `radius * (1 - hardness)`
  while `src/engine/retouch.ts` used `radius * hardness` — so 100 % hardness was
  the *softest* brush, and the same slider that made dodge/burn crisp made the
  brush soft. The brush now uses the retouch convention: hardness is the
  fraction of the radius that is fully opaque. A brush at 100 % paints a crisp
  disc, 25 % keeps a soft band, and the two tools agree.
- The pinned test that had encoded the inverted behaviour ("alpha in the soft
  band" at hardness 1) was replaced, and a new test pins the convention itself.

## Verification

- Engine: 65 test files / 677 tests. New `dualbrush.test.ts` proves a
  transparent second-tip half leaves that side unpainted, an all-zero tip paints
  nothing, and a half-size second tip textures only the centre. Mutation probe:
  disabling the dual sample fails three of those tests; restoring the inverted
  hardness formula fails the two hardness tests.
- Smoke: the brush options bar is asserted to expose the second-tip control and
  its four choices; removing the control fails the smoke run.
- Gate: `node scripts/ci.mjs` green, including pins and the deterministic build.

## Limits

- The dual tip applies to the brush and eraser discs. The retouch tools
  (dodge/burn/sponge/clone) keep their own single-tip falloff; a dual tip there
  would need their loops to share the dab path first.
- Airbrush build-up (depositing while the pointer rests) is not in this release.
- Custom PNG/JPEG tips can be used as the *first* tip; the second tip offers the
  built-in tips only.
